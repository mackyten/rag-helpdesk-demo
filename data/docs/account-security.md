# Account Security and Password Reset

## Resetting your password

Choose "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and enter your account email. We send a reset link that expires after 30 minutes and can be used once. If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder and make sure you used the email address registered on the account.

## Password rules

Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and cannot match any of your last 5 passwords. We lock the account for 15 minutes after 5 failed sign-in attempts in a row.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication is optional for personal accounts and required for workspace administrators. Supported methods are authenticator apps and hardware security keys. SMS codes are not supported. When you enable two-factor authentication we show 10 one-time recovery codes; store them somewhere safe, because support cannot disable two-factor authentication without verifying ownership of the account.

## Signing out everywhere

Open Security settings and choose "Sign out of all devices" to end every active session. Doing this also revokes all personal API tokens.
