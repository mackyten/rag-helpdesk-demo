# Plans and Billing

## Plans

Brightbox has three plans. Starter is free and includes 5 GB of storage and 1 workspace member. Team costs 12 dollars per member each month, or 120 dollars per member each year, and includes 1 TB of storage. Business costs 25 dollars per member each month and adds single sign-on, audit logs and a 99.9 percent uptime commitment.

## Upgrades and downgrades

Upgrades apply immediately and we charge a pro-rated amount for the rest of the billing period. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal. If a downgrade leaves you above the storage limit of the new plan, uploads are blocked until you delete files or upgrade again; existing files are never deleted automatically.

## Payment methods

We accept major credit and debit cards and PayPal. Bank transfers are available only on annual Business plans. Invoices are emailed on the billing date and can be downloaded as PDF from the Billing page.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails we retry after 3 days, then after 7 days. The account moves to read-only mode after the third failure and is suspended 30 days later.

## Taxes

Prices exclude sales tax and VAT. Tax is calculated from the billing address at checkout.
