# Data Privacy and Export

## Data location

Customer data is stored in data centres in the United States and the European Union. Workspace owners on the Business plan can choose the region when the workspace is created; the choice cannot be changed afterwards.

## Exporting your data

Open Settings, choose "Export data" and pick a format: JSON or CSV. The export covers files, comments and member lists. Exports are prepared in the background and the download link is emailed to the workspace owner and stays valid for 7 days.

## Deleting your account

Deleting an account removes personal data within 30 days. Backups are purged within a further 60 days. Invoices and tax records are kept for 7 years because the law requires it.

## Retention of deleted files

Deleted files stay in the trash for 30 days and can be restored by any workspace administrator. After that they are permanently removed and cannot be recovered.

## Sharing with third parties

We never sell customer data. We share data only with the sub-processors listed on our trust page, such as our cloud hosting and payment providers, under data processing agreements.
