# Refund Policy

Brightbox offers a 30-day money-back guarantee on every new subscription. To request a refund, open the Billing page, choose "Request refund" and select the invoice. Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

## What is not refundable

Renewals are refundable only within 7 days of the renewal date. Add-ons such as extra storage packs and priority support are not refundable once they have been used. Gift cards and promotional credits are never refunded as cash.

## Annual plans

If you cancel an annual plan after the first 30 days, we refund the unused full months on a pro-rata basis, minus a 10 percent administration fee. Monthly plans are not pro-rated; you keep access until the end of the paid month.

## Disputes

If you believe you were charged in error, contact support within 60 days of the charge and include the invoice number. We will respond within 2 business days.
