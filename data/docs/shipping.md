# Shipping and Delivery

Brightbox hardware orders ship from our warehouse within 2 business days of payment. Standard shipping takes 5 to 7 business days within the continental United States and costs a flat 6 dollars; orders above 75 dollars ship free.

## Express and international

Express shipping takes 2 business days and costs 18 dollars. International orders are delivered in 10 to 15 business days. Import duties and taxes are charged by the destination country and are the customer's responsibility.

## Tracking

A tracking number is emailed as soon as the parcel leaves the warehouse. If a parcel shows no movement for 7 days, contact support and we will open a carrier investigation.

## Damaged or missing items

Report damaged or missing items within 14 days of delivery with a photo of the packaging. We send a replacement at no cost, or issue a refund if the item is out of stock.

## Address changes

You can change the delivery address only before the order ships. After that, ask the carrier to redirect the parcel.
