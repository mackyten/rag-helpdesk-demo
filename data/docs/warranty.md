# Hardware Warranty

All Brightbox hardware includes a 2-year limited warranty from the delivery date. The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use.

## What is covered

We repair or replace a defective device at our discretion. Replacement units may be refurbished and carry the remaining warranty period or 90 days, whichever is longer.

## What is not covered

Accidental damage, liquid damage, unauthorized repairs, modified firmware and normal wear such as scratches are not covered. Batteries are covered for 1 year, not 2, because capacity declines with use.

## Making a claim

Start a claim from the Support page with your order number and the device serial number found on the underside of the unit. If we approve the claim we email a prepaid return label within 1 business day. Repairs usually take 7 to 10 business days from the day we receive the device.

## Extended warranty

Brightbox Care extends coverage to 4 years and adds one accidental-damage repair per year for a 12 dollar monthly fee. It must be purchased within 60 days of delivery.
